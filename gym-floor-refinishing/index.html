Gym floors for schools, districts and rec facilities

A gym floor takes more wear than almost any floor in a building, and the finish is what protects it. We sand and refinish wood gym floors for schools, districts and athletic facilities, with the work scheduled around your calendar so the floor is ready when you need it.

What we do

  • Screen and recoat: refresh the topcoat on a floor that is worn but sound
  • Full sand and refinish: take the floor to bare wood and refinish it when the finish or surface is badly worn
  • Repairs: address damaged or loose boards before finishing
  • Game lines and logos: tell us what you need and we will scope it with the refinishing schedule

How we work with districts

  1. Tell us about the floorApproximate size, wood type if you know it, when it was last refinished, and any problem areas.
  2. Site visit and written bidWe look at the floor and send a clear written bid.
  3. ScheduleWe plan the job for summer or another break, including cure time before the floor goes back into use.
  4. Work and walk-throughThe job is done with dust and mess kept to a minimum, followed by a walk-through and care instructions.

Frequently asked questions

Can you work around our school calendar?

Yes. Gym floor work is usually planned for summer or other breaks so the floor has time to cure before students return. Tell us your window in your bid request and we will confirm what fits.

What is the difference between a screen and recoat and a full sand on a gym floor?

A screen and recoat refreshes the topcoat and is the lighter, quicker maintenance option when the finish is worn but the wood is sound. A full sand and refinish removes the old finish and surface damage down to bare wood and is the bigger job. We will recommend one after looking at the floor.

Do you provide written bids for school districts?

Yes. Send the floor size, wood type if known, the last time it was refinished, and your target dates, and we will respond with a written bid.

Can you redo game lines and logos?

Tell us what you need in your bid request and we will scope it with the refinishing schedule. Court layouts and paint need to be planned before the final coats.

Do you work outside Chelan and Wenatchee?

Yes. We are based in Chelan and serve central and western Washington. Send us the location and we will let you know.
