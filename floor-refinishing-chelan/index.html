Floor refinishing in Chelan, WA

Max Flooring NW LLC is based in Chelan. We sand and refinish hardwood floors in homes, cabins and businesses around Lake Chelan, and we can be at your door quickly for an estimate.

Wood floors in a dry climate with warm summers and cold winters see seasonal changes, from sun on the boards to wet boots in winter. Worn finish, dull spots and scratches show up first at doors, in kitchens and along walkways, and a fresh finish protects the wood underneath.

What we do in Chelan

  • Screen and recoat
