Frequently asked questions

What is LVP flooring?

LVP stands for luxury vinyl plank. It is a durable, water-resistant flooring that looks like wood and is a popular choice for kitchens, baths, basements and busy homes.

Can you remove my old flooring?

Yes. We remove and haul away old flooring and prep the subfloor before the new floor goes in.

Do you install engineered hardwood?

Yes. We install engineered wood in click, glue-down and floating systems, as well as solid hardwood, pre-finished or site-finished.
